STBPlay

Data safety

A plain summary of how STBPlay handles data. Published by LoopX Studio. The full privacy policy has the details.

At a glance

  • The publisher does not collect your personal data. There is no STBPlay account, no publisher server, no advertising and no analytics.
  • The publisher does not share your data with third parties.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. STBPlay never sees or stores your card or payment details.
  • You can delete your local data at any time in Settings.

What stays on your device

DataWhyWhere
Source profiles, provider login details, favorites, recent viewing, playback positions, preferencesSo the app works between sessionsPrivate app storage on your device. Sensitive fields are encrypted with a key protected by Android Keystore. Excluded from cloud backup.
Free-trial start dateSo the trial does not restart after a reinstallPrivate app storage and Android Block Store on your device. Never sent to the publisher.
Optional app PINTo lock the appA salted verifier is stored, not the PIN itself.

What goes to other parties

  • Your chosen provider and its content servers: login details, requests for channels or titles, your IP address and normal technical headers, so playback can work. These are independent services with their own policies.
  • Google Play: handles purchase of the optional STBPlay Pass under Google's own terms.

Security practices

  • HTTPS is used whenever your provider supports it. The app does not disable certificate checks and does not silently follow an HTTPS-to-HTTP redirect. Plain HTTP sources are not encrypted in transit.
  • Sensitive stored data is encrypted on the device (AES-GCM, Android Keystore). This does not protect a rooted or compromised device.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising-ID permissions are requested.

Deleting your data

  • Settings: Clear watch history or Clear all local data.
  • Or clear storage in Android's app settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Your provider account and any server-held data are managed by that provider.
